10. Too much confidence can be as dangerous as having none at all

13. Conserve your resources

20. [Courage is a full-time job](./chapters/chapter-11)

22. [Always be aware of your surroundings](./chapters/chapter-14)
23. There's nothing wrong with careful curiosity
24. [Take care of your environment, so it can take care of you](./chapters/chapter-15)
